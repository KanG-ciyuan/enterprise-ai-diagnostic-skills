# Adaptive Employee Interview Method

## Operating Rule

Ask one question per turn. Use the smallest number of questions that produces an accurate, employee-confirmed workflow card. Ten to fifteen main questions is a ceiling. Follow-ups replace irrelevant main questions; they do not accumulate mechanically.

## Six Stages

### 1. Select One Job

Capture department, role, job name, frequency, trigger, and consumer. If the job is broad, narrow it to one repeatable output, such as “prepare the weekly sales review report,” not “manage sales operations.”

### 2. Reconstruct The Normal Path

Ask the employee to start from the actual incoming event or material and describe actions in order. For every step capture actor, input, action, system/material, output, and next recipient.

When the employee uses vague verbs such as “整理、处理、跟进、审核、分析、同步,” ask what operations those words contain. Offer neutral examples only after an open question fails.

### 3. Trace Systems And Information

Identify where information originates, where it is copied, re-entered, calculated, searched, transformed, stored, and handed off. Record unofficial files, chat messages, personal notes, and manual workarounds without judging them.

### 4. Expose Decisions And Exceptions

For every judgment, ask what fields, rules, examples, or experience inform it; what cannot be decided alone; and who has final authority. Obtain at least one realistic exception and how the employee recovers or escalates.

### 5. Estimate Burden And Consequence

Capture frequency, typical and peak volume, active work time, waiting time, rework, common omissions, and consequences. Preserve estimates as estimates unless supported by records.

### 6. Evidence And Confirmation

Invite optional desensitized evidence. Produce a concise draft workflow, then ask: “哪一步不准确、遗漏或顺序不对？” Apply corrections and request explicit confirmation.

## Follow-Up Triggers

| Signal | Next question goal |
|---|---|
| vague action | break it into observable operations |
| “看情况” | identify signals, bounds, and escalation |
| “系统自动” | identify trigger, result, and failure behavior |
| multiple actors | identify handoff content, timing, and acceptance |
| time/volume estimate | mark estimate and ask whether a record exists |
| “一般没问题” | request one recent exception |
| policy differs from practice | preserve both and mark conflict |

## Stop Rules

Stop asking when additional answers would not materially change the step sequence, decision points, exception path, handoffs, burden, evidence status, or open questions. Do not interrogate personal performance, employee sentiment, or unrelated company issues.

## Pause And Resume

When the employee pauses, summarize only collected facts and the next unanswered topic. On resume, restate the job and last confirmed step before asking one next question.
